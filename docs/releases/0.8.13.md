oats 0.8.13 improves recording reliability when saving audio temporarily falls behind.

- A temporary audio-saving backlog no longer puts the whole recording into "Recording needs attention" or forces you to start a new note.
- Recording has more buffering, with a separate 32 MiB limit on queued audio samples. If audio is lost, a warning appears while recording and Stop remain available.
- Missing audio is tracked separately for each source. Short gaps remain silence in aligned playback, including gaps at the end of a recording.
- Repeated recoverable device errors can recover without stopping durable recording. If system-audio recovery fails while the microphone continues, the app warns without marking the entire recording as failed.
- Genuine writer, file-saving and nonrecoverable device failures still stop recording and retain available source audio for recovery.

This update preserves notes, recordings, models and the chosen data location. Audio already lost in an earlier recording cannot be reconstructed.

Validation includes deterministic saving-backlog, overflow, recovery and real write-failure checks; recording-state and frontend warning checks; and the Windows app build. Live microphone, system-audio and Bluetooth acceptance remain unverified.
